# Large ranges of prime pairs at every binary exponent

Put $N=1+2^k p q$ for odd primes $p<q$ and an integer $k\ge1$.

**Finite theorem with unrestricted exponents.** Among the 1,103,805 pairs with $p\le1024,\ q\le65536$, the full three-row conjecture holds at every exponent for at least 1,103,107 pairs. It holds for **every pair** in that range at every $k\le10^{12}$, and at every factorial exponent $k=j!$, $j\ge1$. Only 272 pairs remain at unrestricted prime exponents.

**A stronger single-row assertion on a wider range.** If $p\le8191,\ q\le131072,\ k\le512$, every positive trinomial coefficient of weight $N$ shares a prime with $N-1$. This covers 12,052,872 prime pairs and 6,171,070,464 weights. The [three-factor assignment](endpoint_factors.md) and the [all-weight endpoint theorem](all_weights_endpoint_reduction.md) therefore give the full conjecture at each weight.

## Binary shapes and the assigned weight prime

Write $P=2^k,\ m=pq$. Any row avoiding all three predecessor primes has, up to permutation, parts
$$
 P x,\quad P y,\quad P z+1,\qquad x=pa,\quad y=qb,\quad z=m-x-y.
$$
The full powers $P,p,q$ have their exceptional residue one in three separate parts, since a part divisible by $N-1$ exceeds the largest possible part. Thus $P x\equiv1\bmod q,\ P y\equiv1\bmod p$. In particular $0<a<q,\ 0<b<p$. The three binary masks $x,y,z$ are disjoint and sum to $m$. The case $z=0$ would require $Pz+1=1$ to be divisible by $pq$.

In a counterexample this row also avoids some prime $r\mid N$ whose full power $w=r^{v_r(N)}$ divides all three parts. Indeed the product of the weight powers assigned to the row is at least three. Since $N$ is odd and coprime to $pq$, $r$ is odd, $w\mid\gcd(a,b)$, and $P\equiv-m^{-1}\bmod w$.

The first enumeration chooses the submask $y$ and then a proper submask $x$ of $m-y$. The second chooses $t=x+y$, a proper submask of $m$, and reconstructs $b=(tq^{-1}\bmod p)$ and $x=t-qb$. Both require an odd prime dividing $\gcd(a,b)$ and membership of $y\bmod p$ in the group generated by 2. Subtracting two disjoint binary masks here is exact.

The next digits require $[Pa]_p\le[Pq]_p$ and $[Pb]_q\le[Pp]_q$, where brackets denote least nonnegative residue. For instance the $p^1$ digit of $Px$ cannot exceed that of $N$. At $r$ require $[Px/w]_r+[Py/w]_r<r$. Discrete logarithms in the cyclic subgroups generated by 2, and ordinary CRT with possibly noncoprime periods, give each candidate progression $k=e\bmod H$.

## Certifying whole progressions

For each required base $s\in\{r,p,q\}$, $s\mid2^H-1$. At $F=s^{v_s(2^H-1)}$ the scale $P$ has a fixed prefix. When $s=r$, extend the modulus to at least $rw$ and enumerate the entire orbit of that prefix. Discard it if $rw\mid mP+1$. Test every prefix at every layer up to the modulus. The three least residues must sum below that layer.

At a permissible prefix $v\bmod W$, the outstanding multiplication carries are
$$
 (\lfloor xv/W\rfloor,\lfloor yv/W\rfloor,\lfloor(zv+1)/W\rfloor).
$$
Every further base-$s$ digit is allowed as input. For digit $d$, add $(xd,yd,zd)$ to these carries. Discard the transition if the three units digits sum to at least $s$, then divide each entry by $s$ with integer floors. A finite admissible input must reach $(0,0,0)$ after enough leading zero digits. Exhaustion without that state rules out every exponent on the line. Reaching the work limit retains the line. Rejections number 2,710 at $r$, 18 at $p$, and 1 at $q$.

For the 811 remaining lines refine the exponent modulo increasing powers of $r$. When $s^f\parallel 2^H-1$ at an odd $s$, the order of $2^H$ modulo $s^{f+j}$ equals $s^j$, by the binomial theorem. Each new $r$-digit splits a class into exactly $r$ index classes. The primary verifier tests 47,361 individual exponents after lifting. The independent verifier stops lifting earlier and tests 146,592 individual exponents by factorial floors. Both find an obstructing carry or an excess valuation in every case. The independent verifier also reconstructs all 3,540 classes and checks all 2,729 unbounded exclusions by extending actual scalar prefixes and using factorial floors.

For a prime exponent $k$, any progression with $\gcd(e,H)>1$ forces $k\mid H$. All these periods are below $10^{12}$. Only 315 progressions at 272 pairs have $\gcd(e,H)=1$. For factorials compute $j!\bmod H$ successively, stopping when the residue is zero. The progression has $e\ne0$, since $P\equiv1\bmod m$ would force $x+y\equiv1\bmod m$ despite $2\le x+y<m$. No $j!$ above $10^{12}$ meets a remaining class.

## Direct verification through exponent 512

For a fixed $p,q,k$, both $a=(Pp)^{-1}\bmod q$ and $b=(Pq)^{-1}\bmod p$ are forced even without any weight assignment. Start at $k=0$ with $qb=1\bmod p$ and $pa+qb=m+1$, then halve each residue in its field for each successive exponent. Accept only disjoint submasks $x,y$ of $m$. Exactly 1,455 rows survive. A separate program recomputes the three CRT parts directly and applies Legendre's factorial floors using their actual integer values. In 1,449 rows both $p,q$ divide the coefficient. In the other six $p$ divides it. Even coefficients already share 2 with $N-1$.

## Reproduction

The programs take a C++17 compiler and Python 3.9 or later. The line files contain every surviving shape or exponent progression. Threads affect the order of the shape files.

~~~sh
mkdir -p .work
c++ -O3 -std=c++17 -pthread check_odd_predecessor_shapes.cpp -o .work/shapes
c++ -O3 -std=c++17 -pthread check_odd_predecessor_masks.cpp -o .work/masks
.work/shapes 1024 65536 8 > .work/odd-shapes.txt
.work/masks 1024 65536 8 > .work/odd-masks.txt
LC_ALL=C sort .work/odd-shapes.txt > .work/odd-sorted-shapes.txt
LC_ALL=C sort .work/odd-masks.txt > .work/odd-sorted-masks.txt
cmp .work/odd-sorted-shapes.txt .work/odd-sorted-masks.txt
python3 check_odd_predecessor_powers.py .work/odd-shapes.txt > .work/odd-lines.txt
python3 check_odd_predecessor_lifts.py .work/odd-lines.txt --cap 15000 --bound 1000000000000 --exclusions .work/odd-rejected.txt > .work/odd-remaining.txt
python3 check_odd_predecessor_cross.py .work/odd-masks.txt .work/odd-lines.txt .work/odd-remaining.txt .work/odd-rejected.txt
c++ -O3 -std=c++17 -pthread check_odd_predecessor_direct.cpp -o .work/odd-direct
.work/odd-direct 8191 131072 512 8 > .work/odd-direct.txt
python3 check_odd_predecessor_direct.py .work/odd-direct.txt
~~~
